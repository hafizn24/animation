# Buluh FC — Production Rules (ALL EPISODES)

These rules are mandatory for every episode. They exist so the series keeps the same storytelling structure, pacing, subtitle design and cinematic quality as Episode 1. `PRODUCTION_RULES.md` is the single source of truth; the main project guide (`README.md`) and the story docs (`remotion/story/`) link back to it.

---

## 0. 0–2s Pattern-Interrupt Hook Standard (MANDATORY)

Episode 2 held 42% completion but only 28.8% Stayed-to-Watch because it opened on slow exposition. Every episode from Episode 3 onward MUST interrupt the pattern in the first two seconds:

- **Frame 0 is already bright and moving.** Start at full brightness with an active camera push / subject in motion. Never fade in from black, never hold a static wide establishing shot.
- **No backstory, no numbers, no "Previously on…".** Open in media res on the clearest action or conflict beat. Context comes after the hook.
- **First cut or major angle shift by 1.5–2.0s (frames 45–60).** The opening shot must not sit unchanged past frame 60.
- **Frame-0 audio transient.** Trim leading silence from the first TTS clip (`_01`) so voice/action lands at exactly t = 0.0s, and fire a sharp SFX transient on frame 0.
- **Bold hook overlay for the first 1.5s (frames 0–45).** A 3–5 word `HookTitle` spring-pop message must appear inside `SafeArea`, clear of the YouTube Shorts dead zones (bottom 25% / right 15%) and clear of subtitles and the scoreboard HUD.
- Analytics guardrail: the hook must be visible and legible on a phone before the viewer can decide to scroll. Keep the runtime in the 40–45s sweet spot unless the episode's approved story plan justifies a different runtime.

## 1. Script-First Workflow (MANDATORY)

No scene may be built before its script exists.

For every episode, create both of these before touching any Three.js / Remotion code:

- `episodes/episode-X/script.md` — human-readable scene breakdown, timings, visuals, SFX/BGM notes and subtitle text.
- `episodes/episode-X/audio-script.json` — machine-readable source of truth for TTS generation and timed subtitles (lines, speakers, start/end frames).

The `SCENES` array in `remotion/src/EpisodeX/EpisodeXComposition.jsx` and the TTS manifest in `remotion/scripts/generate_tts.py` must mirror `audio-script.json`. When in doubt, the JSON wins.

---

## 2. Multi-Voice Structure (MANDATORY)

Every episode MUST alternate between NARRATOR lines (scene-setting, internal monologue, emotional beats) and CHARACTER DIALOGUE from named characters. A narrator-only episode is rejected. Aim for at least 2 named characters per episode and at least one exchange between them.

---

## 3. Casual Malay Tone (CRITICAL)

All dialogue and narration MUST be natural, casual spoken Malaysian Malay (Bahasa Melayu santai).

- DO use: `tak berbaloi`, `boleh la`, `macam mana`, `ambik la`, `je`, `dah`, `kat`, `akak`, `bro`, `memang`, `sikit`, `tengok`.
- DO NOT use stiff/formal register: `ia`, `beliau`, `apakah`, `walaubagaimanapun`, `mengambil`, `menuju`, `sesungguhnya`, `tertanya-tanya`.
- Keep sentences short and punchy. One idea per line.

---

## 4. Episode 1 Dual-Subtitle System (MANDATORY)

Render subtitles with `remotion/src/components/Subtitles.jsx`. Two distinct styles, never mixed:

| Type | Style |
|---|---|
| **Narration** | Italic, warm gold (`#d8b26a`), serif (Georgia), at the bottom of frame. No speaker label. |
| **Character dialogue** | Clean white sans-serif (Arial/Helvetica), with an uppercase gold speaker label above the text (e.g. `PEMILIK`, `PENAJA 1`, `KEK MAJU`, `JURULATIH`). |

Both sit inside a subtle dark rounded plate so they stay readable on phone screens. Narration is timed per line, not a permanent overlay. For the 1080x1920 Episode 5 master, `remotion/src/Episode5/components/Episode5Subtitles.jsx` is the same design scaled 1.5x.

---

## 5. NO Engagement-Bait CTAs (STRICT)

Never include like / comment / subscribe / "komen episod seterusnya" prompts.

Every episode ends on a dramatic final line, then a hard cut to a clean black title card:

```
Buluh FC
────────────── (thin gold divider)
EPISOD X
```

No CTA. No credits crawl. Hold the card briefly, then end.

---

## 6. Audio-Driven Pacing (MANDATORY)

- Scene and shot durations are locked to the audio script timings. Frames = seconds x FPS (30).
- Maximum 0.5–0.8s buffer between audio lines.
- ZERO 5–9 second silent gaps. If a beat feels long, cut it or fill it with narration — never leave dead air.
- Generate TTS with the project script from `remotion/`.
- Voice mapping lives in `generate_tts.py`; each distinct character gets a distinct voice where possible.
- SFX and BGM cues are procedural beds generated by `python scripts/generate_audio_beds.py`.

---

## 7. Camera & Animation Standards

- Never repeat the same static camera angle more than twice in a scene. Use smooth eased push-ins, pans, cutaways and over-the-shoulder shots.
- Characters are faceless: exaggerate body language — head shakes on Y axis, nods, shoulder slumps, hand gestures, subtle idle breathing.
- No stiff A-poses. No prop clipping. Give distinct characters distinct hair, outfit colours and proportions.
- Keep environments distinct. Never reuse the Buluh FC office as a bakery or sponsor office. No outdoor signposts indoors.
- Use `<SafeArea />` and `<CutFlash />` in every scene sequence.

---

## 8. Definition of Done

Before an episode is called finished:

- [ ] `script.md` and `audio-script.json` exist and match the composition.
- [ ] Narrator and character lines alternate; no narrator-only stretches.
- [ ] All copy is casual Malay; no formal register, no CTA.
- [ ] Dual subtitles render correctly.
- [ ] Final line hard-cuts to the black `Buluh FC / EPISOD X` title card.
- [ ] No silent gap over 0.8s; no repeated static angle; no clipping.
- [ ] `npm run studio` opens the composition; a still render succeeds.
- [ ] Story docs match the current canon and roadmap.
