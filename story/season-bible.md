# Buluh FC — Division 5 Title Run: Season Bible

## Logline
A debt-stricken Divisyen 5 club trades a 30-day death sentence for a season-long instalment plan. Buluh FC must turn RM331,500 balloon into a title — paying RM20,000 every month-end from a kitty built out of wins, draws, gate money and community favours — while a land developer waits for one missed payment to take the stadium.

## Tone
Casual Malaysian Malay, dialogue-led and funny under pressure. The narrator bridges at most two lines per episode on a voice no speaker in that episode shares. Numbers live in the HUD (`BULAN n • MD m`, `HUTANG`, `KITI`), never in throwaway dialogue — a figure is spoken only when it is the joke or the decision. Every set is visually distinct, every shot face-forward, every episode ends on a hard cut to the 72-frame `Buluh FC / EPISOD X` card. No CTA. Karim is gone: no cameo, no call, no letter, no reunion tease. His gold armband sits on Bos's desk until Azam takes it in Ep 8.

## Arc map (22 episodes, 14 matchdays)

### Arc 1 — Freeze (Ep 6–11, MD1–4)
**Ep 6 — Janji Bank.** Open on a live training block, not a meeting: Azam shouts a defensive instruction, the ball breaks, cut. Peguam explains inside the first 25 seconds that the lender would rather be paid monthly than own an empty stadium, so the 30-day countdown is retired and the freeze holds only while an instalment lands at each month-end. Karim's gold armband is established on Bos's desk with no explanation. Manaf (#1) and Arif (#9) arrive with distinct voices and a distinct body-language gag. MD1 at home to Tanjung Rhu, 3–1. Hard cut on the end card.

**Ep 7 — Gaji Lima Ringgit.** MD2 away at Kota Setia, 2–0. Iskandar (#8) quietly takes Karim's old midfield role; the RM5 salary joke gets a fresh punchline when the away crowd hears it. Boss faces the kitty math in the club office; the numbers stay on screen.

**Ep 8 — Kapten #7.** MD3 at home to Pandan Jaya, 1–1. Bos hands the gold armband to Azam in the tunnel before kick-off, settling the captaincy early. Syukur (#4) and Nazir (#3) get their first named lines. The first kitty scare arrives with the draw income.

**Ep 9 — Blok Terakhir.** MD4 away at Sungai Lembing, 2–1, sealed by a last-minute Azam block. The new squad finally gels: one gesture, one huddle, one shared laugh. Hulubalang's unbeaten start is flagged on the league table overlay.

**Ep 10 — Bulan Pertama.** Off-pitch bridge in the new bank-branch set. Peguam walks Bos through the instalment clause and the cure window; Bos counts the kitty with Rizal. No match, no HUD out loud.

**Ep 11 — Bayar Pertama.** Instalment 1 is paid: RM20,000 leaves the kitty, debt drops to RM311,500. The armband stays on Azam's arm. Month 1 closes on a quiet, confident cut.

### Arc 2 — Antagonist (Ep 12–17, MD5–8)
**Ep 12 — Bilik Lawan.** MD5 at home to Bukit Bintang, 3–0. The new rival-clubhouse set introduces Hulubalang captain Shahrol, whose clipboard mockery gives the title race a face. The antagonist makes his first real move off-pitch.

**Ep 13 — Sampul Hitam.** MD6 away at Cerana Rovers, 1–1. A squad member is tempted away with a better-paid offer; Bos answers with the kitty, not a speech. The press room is teased for the next beat.

**Ep 14 — Press Room.** MD7 away at Tanjung Rhu, 2–1. The new press-room set: the antagonist feeds a headline about Buluh's "instalment circus"; Hafiz (#6) turns it into a dressing-room chant.

**Ep 15 — Top Up.** MD8 at home to Kota Setia, 1–2 — the first home loss. Nexus Sports adds a RM1,000 mid-season top-up with strings attached; Penaja smiles through it. Kitty barely covers month 2.

**Ep 16 — Tawaran.** Off-pitch. The tempted squad member almost signs; Azam's RM5 salary becomes the reason to stay. No match, no HUD number spoken.

**Ep 17 — Bayar Kedua.** Instalment 2 is paid; debt drops to RM291,500. Hulubalang still lead the table by four points. Month 2 closes.

### Arc 3 — The fall (Ep 18–22, MD9–11)
**Ep 18 — Padang Kampung.** The new kampung training-ground set; the community turns up with a wooden goal and free teh. MD9 away at Pandan Jaya, 3–0 — the last comfortable win.

**Ep 19 — Tiang Letup.** The floodlight tower blows; +RM8,000 to the debt. MD10 at home to Sungai Lembing, 0–0. The kitty dries up in front of everyone.

**Ep 20 — Kad Merah.** A disciplinary fine adds RM4,000. MD11 away at Bukit Bintang, 1–2. The squad rift breaks open in the tunnel; Azam's captaincy is tested.

**Ep 21 — Baiki Stadium.** Stadium repair adds RM6,000. Debt reaches RM309,500. The bank-branch set returns: Peguam starts the seven-day cure clock, once only.

**Ep 22 — Duit Akak.** MD off. Kek Maju's community fund arrives on day 7, the kitty reaches RM20,000, instalment 3 is paid and the freeze survives. Debt: RM289,500. Kek Maju's "akak sayang kelab kita" line closes the arc.

### Arc 4 — Champions (Ep 23–27, MD12–14)
**Ep 23 — All In.** MD12 at home to Cerana Rovers, 2–1. The squad agrees that every remaining match is a final. Hulubalang's lead is four points.

**Ep 24 — Final Venue.** The new final-day venue set opens. MD13 away at Hulubalang, 3–0 — Buluh leapfrog on goal difference but Hulubalang still lead by one point. Shahrol's clipboard finally cracks.

**Ep 25 — Senarai.** Off-pitch. The table, the head-to-head rule and the goal-difference tiebreak are laid out visually. Nobody says the numbers aloud; the suspense is in the arithmetic.

**Ep 26 — Bayar Terakhir.** Instalment 4 is paid; debt drops to RM269,500. One match left, one point needed, and the wallet is empty. HUD reads `BULAN 4 • MD14`.

**Ep 27 — Juara.** MD14 at home to Hulubalang United, 2–0. Buluh finish on 30 points to Hulubalang's 28 and take the Division 5 title. Champion Prize RM75,000 plus Nexus Sports promotion bonus RM200,000 land: LUNAS, with RM5,500 left over — the RM5 salary joke finally pays out. Final line, hard cut, EndCard. No CTA.

## Antagonist options (Gate 1 decision)
| Option | Pro | Con |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Land developer holding an option on the stadium site** — profits if the freeze breaks | Ties the villain directly to the instalment stakes; keeps Peguam a neutral clock-watcher; gives each month a reason to panic | Needs one new rig and one new set; less of a football rival |
| **B. Owner of Hulubalang United** | Instant, personal title-race antagonist; reuses an established club | Turns a money story into a club feud; muddies the theme; predictable |
| **C. League official weaponising licence rules and fines** | Explains the fines and the tiebreak rules dramatically | Bureaucratic villain; no emotional stake in the stadium; weak payoff |

**Recommendation: A.** It keeps the pressure on the instalment, preserves Peguam's neutrality and makes the champion's payout the exact moment the villain loses.
