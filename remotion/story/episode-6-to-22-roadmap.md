# Buluh FC — Episode 6–22 Season Roadmap

> **Planning document, not episode scripts.** This roadmap defines the season-level story beats and continuity targets. Each episode must still follow [`PRODUCTION_RULES.md`](../../PRODUCTION_RULES.md) and must have its own `episodes/episode-X/script.md` and `audio-script.json` before any scene code is written.
>
> **Canon baseline:** Buluh FC enters this season with **RM326,500 outstanding debt** after applying Kek Maju's RM5,000 contribution to the RM331,500 post-Episode-5 balance. Karim (#8) has departed, Azam (#7) has joined on RM5 salary, wage costs are down 35%, and the captaincy is vacant.

## Season premise

Buluh FC must keep its stadium and earn promotion while carrying RM326,500 in debt. The lawyer grants a conditional extension: **stadium seizure is frozen until the end of the Division 5 season**, subject to a written agreement. This replaces the old immediate countdown as the active season pressure. The debt remains real and must not disappear simply because the team wins matches.

The tone is balanced: grassroots football comedy, dressing-room relationships, competitive drama, rivalries and emotional consequences. Keep the football believable and the financial stakes present in the background.

## Division 5 competition format

- **12 clubs** in one league table.
- **Single round-robin:** every club plays every other club once.
- **11 matchdays per club; 66 league matches total.**
- Points: win = 3, draw = 1, loss = 0.
- The club finishing **1st** is champion and earns promotion to Division 4.
- No second leg. Do not add playoffs, extra promotion places or relegation rules unless they are deliberately approved later.
- The other 11 clubs, official club names, kits, captains, squads, grounds and full fixture order are **to be created and locked before the matchday scripts**.
- The series does not need to dramatize all 66 fixtures. Prioritize Buluh FC's match and show selected results/table movement to make the wider league feel alive.

### Financial bookkeeping rules

1. Start Episode 6 at **RM326,500**. The RM5,000 Kek Maju contribution is already deducted.
2. Do not count the RM50,000 Karim transfer twice; it was fully applied to debt before the post-Episode-5 balance of RM331,500.
3. Do not invent a value for the earlier Kek Maju envelope beyond the user's confirmed RM5,000 contribution.
4. Every future debt change must identify its source (payment, prize, sponsor, ticket income, expense, fee) and exact amount before changing the ledger.
5. A league result, promotion or sponsor conversation does not change debt until money is actually awarded, received or paid.

## Development order

1. Create and approve the 12 club identities, colours, home grounds, captains, key players and playing styles.
2. Build the 11-round fixture list and a results/standings tracker before writing matchday scripts.
3. Confirm the written conditions of the lawyer's extension so later episodes do not contradict them.
4. Write Episode 6 script and audio JSON first; then proceed episode by episode.
5. After every matchday episode, update the fixture results and table before planning the next one.
